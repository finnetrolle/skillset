# Landing Audit Rubric

Use this rubric when the task needs a serious critique, a rewrite plan, or direct landing-page edits.

## Fast Pass

Mark each area as `red`, `yellow`, or `green`:

- offer clarity
- audience fit
- differentiation
- proof
- CTA clarity
- section flow
- copy strength

## Offer Clarity

Red flags:

- Reader cannot tell what the product is in five seconds.
- Headline is mood or slogan instead of promise.
- Subhead repeats the headline without adding mechanism or outcome.

Fix direction:

- State product, user, outcome, and mechanism with less abstraction.

## Audience Fit

Red flags:

- Copy speaks to everyone.
- Problem statement is broad or generic.
- Important objections for the likely buyer are missing.

Fix direction:

- Name the user, context, urgency, or use case more precisely.

## Differentiation

Red flags:

- The page could belong to any competitor.
- Benefits sound familiar but not specific.
- The mechanism sounds interchangeable with a generic tool or service.

Fix direction:

- Explain what is unique in approach, speed, guarantee, focus, or delivery.

## Proof And Credibility

Red flags:

- Claims outrun evidence.
- Proof is decorative, vague, or disconnected from the offer.
- No numbers, customer type, process detail, or constraints appear anywhere.

Fix direction:

- Add evidence, mechanism, scope, or honest limitations that make claims believable.

## Hierarchy And Flow

Red flags:

- Sections repeat the same promise in different words.
- CTA appears before trust is earned or disappears after the hero.
- Benefits, features, proof, and objections appear in random order.

Fix direction:

- Rebuild the sequence as promise, explanation, proof, objections, CTA.

## CTA And Conversion

Red flags:

- CTA is generic.
- Next step, timing, or expected outcome is unclear.
- Risk level of the CTA does not match the amount of trust built on the page.

Fix direction:

- Align CTA language with user intent and make the next step concrete.

## Copy Quality

Red flags:

- Filler adjectives, jargon, empty intensifiers, or vague claims.
- Long sentences that bury the point.
- Repetition between headline, subhead, bullets, and later sections.

Fix direction:

- Cut, specify, and tighten before adding new copy.

## Severity Rules

- `P1`: blocks understanding or trust
- `P2`: weakens conversion materially
- `P3`: polish issue with smaller impact
