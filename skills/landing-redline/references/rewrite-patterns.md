# Rewrite Patterns

Use these patterns when the landing message is weak but salvageable.

## Headline

Prefer:

- who it is for
- outcome
- mechanism or differentiator

Pattern:

- `Get [outcome] for [audience] without [friction]`
- `The [product type] for [audience] that [specific differentiator]`

## Subhead

Add what the headline does not:

- how it works
- how fast it works
- why it is credible
- what the CTA leads to

## CTA

Prefer action plus expected result.

Weak:

- `Learn more`
- `Get started`

Stronger:

- `See how the audit works`
- `Book a 15-minute demo`
- `Get the pricing breakdown`

## Proof Block

Replace broad claims with one of:

- number
- customer type
- process detail
- before and after outcome
- scope or constraint

## Closing Section

End with:

- who should act
- what happens next
- why the step is low friction or high value

Do not end with another vague brand slogan.
